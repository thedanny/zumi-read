GOOD TO KNOW

A few parent questions.

Can we try it before signing up?

Yes. Read a complete sample story and try the page-turning reader without an account.

Do questions interrupt the story?

No. Your child reads first and opens the questions after saying they are ready. Parents can also decide whether the story stays closed until questions are finished.

Can I guide the kinds of stories we get?

Yes. Set themes, story length, tone, and story depth. Choose comprehension skills and topics to avoid; your feedback guides later stories.

Can a favorite story continue?

Yes. Start a series to keep familiar characters and their important moments, then send them on a new adventure. You can also make a fresh, standalone story.
